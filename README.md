# Eri's Kink Stuff!

A Minecraft mod designed to provide tools for players to express and fulfill various roleplay scenarios and themes within the world or Minecraft.

## 🔞 Intent & Purpose
This mod is specifically crafted to help players explore specialized roleplay. It adds functional items and furniture that facilitate power dynamics, sensory play, and pet play themes in a multiplayer environment.

## ✨ Features

### 🐕 Player Leashing
The heart of the mod is a system that allows players to be tied together and led around.
* **Collar Requirement:** A player must be wearing a Collar to be leashed.
* **Leash Interaction:** Use a vanilla Lead on a collared player to start leading them.
* **Never Get Lost:** If a leashed player gets stuck behind a wall or get too far away, they will automatically be brought back to their holder's side.

### ⛓️ The Collar
A decorative and functional item worn around the neck.
* **Style:** A visible 3D model that stays on your character's neck.
* **Dyeable:** Can be coloured with any combination of dyes, just like leather armor.
* **Purpose:** The essential starting point for leashing and obedience training.

### 🧤 Mittens (Hand Restraints)
Soft restraints that prevent a player from using their hands.
* **Empty Hands:** While wearing Mittens, you cannot hold any items.
* **Auto-Tuck:** Anything you try to pick up or hold will be put away into your bags, or dropped if you have no room.
* **Visuals:** Your hands will appear covered in both first-person and third-person views.
* **Dyeable:** Can be coloured to match your outfit.

### 🌑 The Blindfold
A tool for sensory play that limits what the wearer can see.
* **Vision Loss:** While worn, your screen will go dark, simulating total blindness.
* **Visuals:** A 3D model that clearly covers your character's eyes.
* **Dyeable:** Can be coloured with dyes.

### 🐱 Cat Ears
Cute ears for feline-themed roleplay.
* **Aesthetic:** Adds cat ears to your character.
* **Dyeable:** The inner and outer parts can be coloured to your liking.

### 🖤 Latex Bodysuit (Catsuit)
A sleek, all-encompassing outfit for a distinct look.
* **Total Coverage:** Wraps your entire character in a smooth texture.
* **Clean Look:** Automatically hides your character's outer clothing layers (like jackets and hats) so the suit fits perfectly.
* **Natural Fit:** Designed to work beautifully with popular character shape mods.
* **Dyeable:** Can be coloured with dyes for a custom look.

### 🔔 The Clicker
A simple tool for training and commands.
* **Attention!:** When used, it plays a sharp sound and forces all nearby collared players to immediately look at you.
* **Dyeable:** Can be coloured to match your style.

### 🛏️ Pet Beds
A comfortable, low-profile bed for pets to rest on.
* **Custom Colours:** Made from wool and carpet, inheriting the colours of the materials used. Can also be dyed further after crafting.
* **Fully Functional:** Acts as a real bed where you can sleep and set your home point.
* **Cozy Design:** A large, futon-style bed that fits perfectly into a pet's corner.

## 🛠️ Requirements
* **[Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/):** *(Version: 1.21.1)*
* **[NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/):** The platform needed to run the mod. *(Version 21.1.228 or higher)*
* **[Curios API](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios):** The mod that allows you to wear the collars, mittens, and ears.

## 💡 Recommendations
* **[Female Gender](https://modrinth.com/mod/female-gender):** Highly recommended to enhance character customization for specific roleplay aesthetics.

## 🎨 Getting Started
Currently most items can be found in the custom "Eri's Kink Stuff" tab in the creative menu.
